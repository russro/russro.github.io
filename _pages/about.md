ML researcher. PhD candidate @ [UC Berkeley Bioengineering](https://bioegrad.berkeley.edu/), with a major emphasis in Computer Science. Advised by [Reza Abbasi-Asl](https://abbasilab.org/). [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) fellow.

I research and build ML and agentic systems. Currently focused on:

* ML for clinical trial design,
* meta-learning on model zoos for sample-efficient LLM profiling,
* and production agentic and ML systems as an ML Engineer (consultant) at [FenixCommerce](https://www.fenixcommerce.com/).

*10/2026*: Our paper "From the Zoo to the BULLPEN: Sample-Efficient LLM Profiling via Multi-Channel Model Embeddings" is under review at **ICLR 2027**. I'm also reviewing for ICLR 2027.

*07/26/2026*: Our paper [RECITE: Revising Eligibility Criteria Incorporating Textual Evidence](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3770855.3819027) was accepted to **KDD 2026** (AI4Sciences track).
