**2025 – present** \| Advisor: [Reza Abbasi-Asl](https://abbasilab.org/) (UCSF / UC Berkeley) \| Related: [RECITE benchmark](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3770855.3819027)

LLM pipelines and retrieval over 500K+ clinical trials for eligibility criteria analysis and patient–trial matching, plus large-scale studies of how criteria evolve and whether trials adopt published recommendations for broadening patient eligibility.

- [RECITE](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3770855.3819027) (KDD 2026): benchmark of 11,913 eligibility criteria revisions across 5,735 trials, built from protocol amendments, with 15 LLM configurations evaluated with and without retrieval-augmented generation
- Eligibility criteria tracking across 500K+ trials with 800K+ version snapshots
- Adoption gap analysis: 19K+ assessments across 658 recommendations and 4,400+ trials
- Paper discovery and extraction from 7,400+ papers (495 with verified recommendations)
- ClintriaLM: LLM pipeline for patient–trial matching and eligibility criteria analysis, using biomedical embedding models for retrieval
